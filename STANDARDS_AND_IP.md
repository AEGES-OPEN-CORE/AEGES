## OCUP integration, interoperability, and standards posture

AEGES is the value-sensitive restriction, quarantine, and release-governance layer now unified within **OCUP — One Chip Unified Protocol**. It complements OCUP’s hardware-enforced Temporal Authority Governance by determining how governed actions and outputs are restricted, withheld, quarantined, or released under externally established policy.

Within the unified OCUP architecture:

* **QSAFP** provides the temporal-authority enforcement foundation.
* **AEGES** provides value-sensitive restriction, quarantine, and release governance.
* **RAAVE** provides complementary hardware-rooted identity, authority-continuity signaling, and noncompliance detection.

These open-core initiatives support inspection, experimentation, independent testing, interoperability, and alternative conforming implementations. They do not require any standards body or evidence framework to mandate OCUP or a single vendor architecture.

Temporal Authority Systems PBC supports a **FRAND-friendly path to standards adoption**. If protected OCUP or RAAVE technology becomes essential to an adopted open standard, our present policy direction is to make applicable essential patent claims available on fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory terms, subject to formal licensing terms and the requirements of the relevant standards process.

Alternative implementations remain welcome where they demonstrably satisfy the same core invariants: externally established authority, non-self-extension, fail-closed operation, trusted continuity, revalidation after material change, and authority applicability at consequential action release.
